Support & privacy

Troubleshoot connection
  • In Starcat, open Settings → Extensions & Connections → Browser Extensions and enable the browser plugin service.
  • Check that the service address matches the one in Starcat and the loopback connection is allowed by your firewall or proxy.
  • After saving a change, refresh the GitHub repository page.
